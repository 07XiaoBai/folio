Boxing Day Sale 2025

Holiday-aligned omni-channel gift-card surge during summer school holidays — Meta, TikTok, and email, with BFCM creatives repurposed for the Boxing Day window.

Involvement: Campaign analysis, campaign ownership, and the Boxing Day creative pack.

How it was won

Strategy
Align a short Boxing Day gift-card sale to peak QLD summer school-holiday demand, reuse proven BFCM creatives, and push hard on final-day urgency across Meta, TikTok, and email.
Audience / targeting

Year-end analysis credits holiday alignment plus creative reuse and urgency messaging for the YoY surge — Funds Received +432.96% with lower ad costs than 2024.

Product launch 8–22 Aug 2025

Summer Pass 2025

Seasonal pass launch ($79 vs $199) with email as a primary revenue driver alongside Meta and TikTok. Largest single product-launch Funds Received figure in this 2025 campaign set.

Involvement: Campaign ownership, reporting, and Summer Pass creative packs.

How it was won

Strategy
Launch a clear seasonal pass product ($79, struck from $199) with a fixed sale window, lean on the CRM list as the primary driver, and support with Meta and TikTok paid.
Audience / targeting
Existing customers and the email database as the primary pool (email order value A$54,362). Paid Meta also used Gold Coast, Brisbane, and warm audiences. Grandparents appeared as a creative theme, not as the only targeting cut.

Year-end report: email was the primary driver; 2,275 passes sold showed strong product–market fit; Facebook held a healthy 8.84 ROAS at ~A$9.8k spend. TikTok lagged (1.48 ROAS) — creative suited Facebook better.

Gift-card / sale event 18 Nov – 2 Dec 2025

BFCM 2025

Scaled Black Friday / Cyber Monday gift-card and sale event with early access (20 Nov) and main sale (28 Nov – 1 Dec). Omni-channel mix of Meta (dominant), TikTok, Klaviyo email, and organic Facebook / Instagram. Early Access contributed ≈ 36.47% of total sales.

Involvement: Campaign strategy, ownership, and the BFCM creative pack.

How it was won

Strategy
Plan weeks ahead, film role-based creatives (announcement / how-it-works / countdown / closing), open with Early Access one week before the main sale, then run deadline email sequences through close.
Audience / targeting
Meta purchase ads used distinct Gold Coast, Brisbane, and sale-retargeting / warm roles (Gold Coast led volume; warm and sale retargeting was highly efficient). Email used broad-list launch and closing sends.
Winning creative
Different creatives for retargeting, countdown, and announcement were the top-performing assets across Meta and TikTok. Meta volume leaders included Gold Coast aerial/offer statics and warm deadline / park-action video. Early Access and “ENDS TONIGHT” urgency statics carried the closing window. TikTok boomerang gift-card styles (e.g. swing drone) topped platform ROAS in the year-end TikTok creative ranking.
Why it worked
Early Access alone drove ~36.47% of sales; email order value grew +167% YoY with the last email a top performer; role-based creatives scaled volume even as Facebook ROAS fell vs 2024’s smaller spend.

Top-of-funnel 2025 school-holiday windows

School-holiday lead & giveaway engine 2025

Full-funnel list growth timed to QLD school holidays — Meta as the primary lead channel, TikTok for traffic, and email nurture afterward. Windows included an International campaign (5 Dec 2024 – 23 Jan 2025), a September giveaway (29 Aug – 18 Sep), and a December giveaway (12 Nov – 1 Dec).

How it was won

Strategy
Time broad-appeal giveaways and an international lead flight to school-holiday demand; use Meta for leads, TikTok for traffic, then nurture via email.
Audience / targeting
Broad consumer / family audiences preparing for school breaks — not niche B2B segments. (Niche Fundraising / Gym / EOFY flights failed with CPLs over A$180 and were explicitly called audience/offer mismatches.)
Winning creative
School-out lifestyle creatives such as “SCHOOL’S OUT, FUN IS IN!” with a Book Now CTA. International flights favoured aerial / music-only video over closed shots and voiceover (Meta creative notes).
Why it worked
Holiday-aligned giveaways held blended CPLs under A$1; International hit A$0.05 CPL during peak summer demand. Meta drove the majority of Sept/Dec leads.
